Astrology of the Barycenter · 1800 – 2200

    How the numbers were made

    Barycenter positions come from NASA JPL's DE440 planetary ephemeris, sampled monthly from January 1800 to December 2200 with Skyfield. The distance shown is |Sun − Solar System Barycenter|, expressed in solar radii (R☉ = 695,700 km).

    Calculation and visualization approach adapted from Lavender Elle Hanson's notebook Solar System Barycenter Calculations. Her stats — barycenter inside the Sun ~38.25 % of the time, within half a solar radius ~6.67 % of the time — are reproduced here almost exactly.

    Nothing on this page is a scientific claim about human affairs. Barycenterology is a poetic frame — a way of noticing that the ground beneath us is, in the largest sense, always in motion.
